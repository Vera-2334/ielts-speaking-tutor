# IELTS Speaking Band Descriptors (Official)

Source: IELTS.org

## Band 9

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Fluency | Fluent, only very occasional repetition/self-correction. Any hesitation is about content, not language. Topic development fully coherent. |
| Lexical | Total flexibility and precise use in all contexts. Sustained accurate and idiomatic language. |
| Grammar | Precise and accurate at all times, apart from native-speaker slips. |
| Pronunciation | Full range of phonological features. Flexible connected speech throughout. Effortlessly understood. |

## Band 8

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Fluency | Fluent, occasional content-related hesitation. Topic development coherent and relevant. |
| Lexical | Wide resource, flexibly used. Skilful use of less common/idiomatic items. Occasional inaccuracies in word choice/collocation. Effective paraphrase. |
| Grammar | Wide range, flexibly used. Majority of sentences error-free. Occasional non-systematic errors. |
| Pronunciation | Wide range of phonological features. Sustains rhythm. Flexible stress/intonation across long utterances. Easily understood. |

## Band 7

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Fluency | Able to keep going and produce long turns without noticeable effort. May demonstrate language-related hesitation, repetition, or self-correction. Uses a range of connectives and discourse markers with some flexibility. |
| Lexical | Resource flexibly used to discuss a variety of topics. Some ability to use less common/idiomatic items, with awareness of style and collocation. Some inappropriate choices. Effective paraphrase. |
| Grammar | Uses a range of complex structures with some flexibility. Frequently produces error-free sentences, though some mistakes persist. |
| Pronunciation | Displays all positive features of Band 6, and some, but not all, of Band 8. |

## Band 6

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Fluency | Willing to produce long turns. Coherence may be lost due to hesitation, repetition, self-correction. Uses discourse markers/connectives but not always appropriately. |
| Lexical | Resource sufficient for extended discussion. Meaning clear despite inappropriacies. Generally able to paraphrase. |
| Grammar | Mix of short and complex sentences. Limited flexibility. Errors frequent in complex structures but rarely impede communication. |
| Pronunciation | Range of phonological features but control is variable. Chunking appropriate but rhythm affected. Some effective intonation/stress but not sustained. Generally understood. |

## Band 5

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Fluency | Keeps going but relies on repetition, self-correction, or slow speech. Hesitates mid-sentence for basic lexis/grammar. More complex speech causes disfluency. |
| Lexical | Sufficient for familiar/unfamiliar topics but limited flexibility. Attempts paraphrase, not always successful. |
| Grammar | Basic sentences fairly well controlled. Complex structures attempted but limited in range, nearly always contain errors. |
| Pronunciation | Displays all positive features of Band 4, and some, but not all, of Band 6. |

## Scoring Principles

1. A candidate must **fully fit all positive features** of the descriptor at a given level
2. Final score = average performance across **all parts** of the test
3. The four criteria are **equally weighted**
